How this works. We check the wind forecast for every spot and show your odds of getting your chosen wind speed, hour by hour. Darker red means good odds, lighter red means unlikely.

Tuned by local knowledge. Some spots have quirky local wind patterns that generic forecasts miss — we adjust for that using real reports from local riders and nearby live wind stations, so the numbers get more accurate over time. Tap "Report actual conditions" on any spot to help tune it further.

Only the good options are listed below. The headline at the top always calls out your single best option for the day; the lists below only include spots with a decent shot at your chosen range, to keep the page short — every spot is still shown on the map above regardless of odds.

This is a forecasting aid, not a safety guarantee — always check current conditions, tides and marine warnings before heading out.
